about

hello. first of all, thanks for stopping by; i appreciate it.

i'm a software developer at Expedia Group, and i want to help take you on your next vacation.

at work, i enjoy leading projects end-to-end, operational excellence that makes for a boring on-call, and building with AI; thoughtfully, frugally, with guardrails.

i'm a computer science grad from the Georgia Institute of Technology. my first job was with the network automation rockstars at Itential, who i'm grateful to for setting my bar on how to work with and respect humans.

outside of corporate, i pass my time deadlifting, fussing over single-origin coffee, and i think i can make cream puffs as good as your nearest bakery's.

work history

2021–present

software developer, Expedia Group

i'm on the Virtual Market Coach team; the coaching system that nudges hotel and vacation-rental partners toward better listings on Partner Central and Vrbo Owner. lately i lead our server-driven UI platform (internally, Blueprint) and the Supply Partner MCP server that exposes coaching tips and inline actions to AI agents.

i've also led the team's accessibility push, a Spring Boot v3 upgrade on the domain service, and our monthly operational excellence reviews. earlier on this same team i was tech lead for the Top Tasks integration and a contributor to the Cars shopping migration onto EGTP.

2019

built early iterations of the Adapter Builder, a low-code tool that turns OpenAPI specs into Node.js network-API adapters; cut adapter development time from 30+ hours to about one. customer-facing work landed in front of Atlassian, VMware, and NetBrain, and got demoed at ONUG.

2018–2019
